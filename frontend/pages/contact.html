Register Your NGO

Your organization is managed from an NGO account, so only your team can edit it and see your volunteers.

  1. Create an NGO account (choose "NGO / Organizer" when signing up) and verify your email.
  2. Open My Dashboard and fill in your organization details (registration number, cause, city).
  3. An admin verifies your organization. Then you can publish drives and campaigns and see who joins.
